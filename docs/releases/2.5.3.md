# Aletheia 2.5.3

Aletheia 2.5.3 closes two desktop layout defects and removes the provider-history failure caused by genuinely irregular observation timestamps.

## Desktop corrections

- The header reserves a fixed four-column action area. `Run Arena` and `Cancel` occupy the same final slot, so the active primary action cannot extend beyond the form edge.
- The Fund Discovery `Load sample` and `Open CSV` actions are compact 100 x 36 pixel controls instead of filling the complete card height.
- The lower action cards retain explicit spacing above the status bar.

## Irregular-history convention

Provider observations are never interpolated, forward-filled or moved to synthetic dates. When the timestamps do not support a regular daily, business-daily, weekly or monthly classification, Aletheia computes an explicit effective cadence:

$$
\mathrm{periodsPerYear}
= \frac{\mathrm{observedIntervals}\cdot365.25}{\mathrm{elapsedCalendarDays}}
$$

That convention is passed explicitly to annualized risk metrics, dynamic-state volatility, periodic-investment scaling and calendar-horizon resolution for current forecasts and walk-forward validation. The original series remains classified as `Irregular`.

## Versioning

- Product version: `2.5.3`
- Scientific version: `2.5.0-irregular-cadence`

The scientific version changes because this release affects annualization and forecast-horizon semantics, not only presentation.
